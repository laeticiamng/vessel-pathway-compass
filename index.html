VASCU-LINK · Vessel Pathway Compass — 4-Zero Vascular Decision Support

VASCU-LINK is a doctoral research program validating a 4-zero vascular decision chain (0 mSv · 0 injected contrast · 0 helium · sober hardware · recycled/biosourced materials) on the certified Philips Ingenia 3T MRI at Hôpital de Moutier (Réseau de l'Arc, Switzerland). Vessel Pathway Compass is the software platform. AquaMR low-field hardware is a post-PhD R&D horizon, referenced through OSI²ONE, MRI4ALL, Mbarara, O'Reilly 2021 (PMID 34907181). Research prototype, not a CE-marked medical device.
